# finx

- All environments (Mac, Codex Cloud and mobile) work on `master`. No preview branches.
- Before editing: `git status --short`, `git fetch origin --prune`, then `git pull --ff-only origin master` with a clean worktree. Preserve uncommitted work.
- Before pushing, fetch again and reconcile concurrent changes without force-pushing or discarding work.
- Keep the public site minimal: white, Apple system fonts, small type, plain links and separate short articles.
- Keep content general, sourced and useful for all 2027 Level I windows. Do not publish personal information or credentials.
- Salary bands must be labeled estimates; verify changing CFA, admissions and hiring facts with primary sources.
- Retain image credits. Label illustrative graphs and assumptions. Keep public README short.
- Use Firefox for browser computer use. Check relevant behavior before pushing.
- Commit format: `<type>: <subject>`, no scope or co-author tag.

- The site has no progress tracker or audio/listening feature. Keep reading times and the CFA planner.
- Preserve the compact 70% desktop presentation and normal readable sizing on phones.

## Visual style
- The signature is thin monochrome linework, generous white space and precise ideas—not a fixed comic template.
- Design each illustration from the article's concept. Use objects, editorial metaphors, maps, diagrams, graphs, environments or occasional people. Vary composition, scale and rhythm.
- Do not default to two panels, two people, speech bubbles or an enclosing rectangular frame. Use them only when the idea specifically needs them.
- An illustration should add a relationship, explanation or memorable insight beyond repeating the adjacent text. Keep embedded wording sparse and legible.
- Use original accessible SVGs; match HTML alt text and captions to the actual scene. Label fictional numbers and illustrative financial scenarios. Graph geometry must match its numbers.
- Keep the finx line monogram in assets/finx-mark.svg and the matching wordmark consistent. No stock photographs or colorful decorative imagery.
- Retain the original backtest comic as one compositional example, not the template for new work.

- Match diagram form to the question: workflow for project review, sequence for evidence handoffs, timeline for publication cutoffs, and explicit yes/no/unknown outcomes for decisions.
- Dense diagrams must stay readable in the 70% desktop view and on phones. Use the keyboard-accessible diagram-scroll region when shrinking would hide labels; prevent page-wide overflow.
- Distinguish illustrative scenario points from a fitted curve or an optimum. Declare unscaled timelines, cutoff meanings and uncertainty branches. Keep stable participants/relationships in SVG metadata when useful.
- Validate and render changed SVGs, inspect the family and individual reading-size images, and verify the actual page in Firefox before pushing. Keep review files outside the public repository.
