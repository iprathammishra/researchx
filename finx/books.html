Books, in a useful order

Do not buy nine books at once. Start with OpenStax or Financial Intelligence, use CFA’s current curriculum for the exam, and add a specialist reference when a project needs it. The reading order below is a recommendation.

WhenBook / sourceAccessHow to use itOutput
StartPrinciples of Finance · OpenStaxFree web/PDFRead statements, time value, bonds, shares and capital budgeting. Solve the exercises before coding a model.Explain how a profitable firm can run short of cash.
Exam spineCFA Program curriculum · 2027Digital access with registration; print options paidUse the learning outcomes and questions for your exam year. General finance books do not replace ethics, syllabus details or exam practice.Keep an error log by learning outcome.
Business judgmentFinancial Intelligence · Berman, Knight & CasePaid / libraryAn accessible manager’s view of financial statements, assumptions and business ratios. Read alongside one real annual report.Explain a margin change to a non-finance manager.
ValuationInvestment Valuation · Aswath DamodaranBook paid; author materials freeUse as a reference after accounting and discounting. Start with cash flows, growth, discount rates and relative valuation.Produce a valuation range and state what changes it.
Portfolio depthInvestments · Bodie, Kane & MarcusPaid / libraryStudy diversification, asset pricing, fixed income and performance. A substantial reference, not another cover-to-cover obligation before Level I.Explain benchmark-relative performance.
Risk specializationOptions, Futures, and Other Derivatives · John HullPaid / libraryBegin with forwards, futures, option payoffs and hedging. Save stochastic models for a role that needs them.Draw a hedge payoff and identify basis risk.
CommunicationStorytelling with Data · Cole Nussbaumer KnaflicBooks paid; site examples freeRead while building your first business presentation. Practice audience, chart choice, visual hierarchy and the requested action.Replace a dashboard tour with one decision slide.
Applied MLMachine Learning for Trading · Stefan JansenBook paid; companion repository publicUse the author’s current repository for project structure and data workflows. Pin your environment; older editions use older APIs.Reproduce a baseline with transaction costs and a clean test split.
Advanced / optionalAdvances in Financial Machine Learning · Marcos López de PradoPaid / libraryRead after you can explain leakage, labels and model selection. Prioritize validation and research design over sophisticated algorithms.Write a backtest audit before increasing model complexity.

A workable reading rule

Read for 30 minutes, solve or model for 30, then explain the result in five sentences. Stop adding resources when the bottleneck is practice. Older editions can teach stable fundamentals, but CFA preparation must match the 2027 learning outcomes.
